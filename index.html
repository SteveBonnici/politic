How this works. Each party gets a 0–100 score per issue for how closely its 2026 platform matches the approaches you ticked (averaged when you tick several). These scores are judgements made from party policy documents, fiscal plans and news coverage as at 17 October 2026. Your tax score comes from the calculations shown. The overall match is the weighted average.

This is a model, not advice. Wealth-tax rules for trusts and private businesses have not been published by the Greens or Te Pāti Māori, and NZ First's $14k tax-free band is unconfirmed for 2026. Check your position with a tax adviser.

Sources: party websites and fiscal plans, KPMG and Deloitte election tax trackers, RNZ, 1News, ODT, The Spinoff, interest.co.nz, Waatea; electorate candidates from the Electoral Commission's 2026 nominations.
